How the estimate works

EVE hands out item IDs in increasing order, so a newer structure has a higher ID. EVE Ref has logged every structure it can see several times a day, and its records go back to November 2016. That gives a first-seen date for about 60,000 IDs.

A first sighting can only be late, never early. So a structure was anchored no later than the first sightings of the IDs above it. The curve takes the fifth-earliest of those sightings rather than the earliest, because IDs are allocated in blocks and the odd higher ID really is a little older than its neighbours.

  • Seen This ID was itself sighted, and its own sighting agrees with its neighbours.
  • Between neighbours Placed between the nearest days on the curve.
  • Before Nov 2016 Older than the data. Only a "no later than" date is possible.
  • Projected Newer than anything sighted yet. Projected from the recent rate of new IDs.

The range starts two days before the earlier neighbour, to allow for the 24-hour anchor timer and the delay before a structure is first seen.
